In 1903, French chemist Édouard Bénédictus accidentally knocked a glass flask
off his laboratory shelf. The flask cracked but did not shatter into pieces,
remaining intact in its original shape. Investigating why, Bénédictus discovered
that the flask had contained a solution of cellulose nitrate which had
evaporated, leaving a thin transparent coating that held the glass together.
This accidental discovery led to the invention of laminated safety glass, now
standard in automobile windshields, preventing countless injuries from
shattered glass.
