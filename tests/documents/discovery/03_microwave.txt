In 1945, Percy Spencer was an engineer at Raytheon working on magnetron radar
technology for the US military. One day while standing near an active radar set,
he noticed that a chocolate bar in his pocket had completely melted. Curious
about this unexpected effect, Spencer placed popcorn kernels near the magnetron
and watched them pop. He then tried an egg, which promptly exploded. Spencer
realized the microwaves were heating food from the inside, leading to the
invention of the microwave oven, which transformed home cooking and became a
staple of kitchens globally.
